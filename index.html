FAQ

Is Sue a real cat?

Very real. A crew in Barberton, Ohio found her in the skid plate of a Komatsu excavator, warmed her up, and kept her on as the shop cat. The photos on this page are hers.

Is this token affiliated with Sue's crew, or with Komatsu?

No. $SUE is an unaffiliated community memecoin. It is not endorsed by, connected to, or operated by the construction crew, the photographer, Komatsu Ltd., or the person who first posted about Sue.

What does $SUE actually do?

It's a memecoin — no product, no business model, no roadmap. The one mechanic it has is the 1% sell tax, which is distributed to holders as BNB. Beyond that, it's a token named after a cat in a hardhat.

How do the BNB rewards work?

Every sell is taxed 1%, and that 1% is distributed to $SUE holders in BNB — real BNB in your wallet, not more $SUE. Buys are untaxed. Rewards scale with how much $SUE you hold and how much selling volume there is, so they're variable and not guaranteed: quiet weeks pay out very little.

How do I avoid buying a fake?

Only ever use the contract address published at the top of this page and pinned on our official X account. Scammers deploy copycat tokens with identical names and logos within minutes of any launch.
